# Phuc's First Night

Phuc opens his eyes. He is on a beach in a big, square world. The sea is blue and the sand is yellow. His hands are empty. He hasn't got any food, any tools or a house.

"Okay," he says. "I need wood."

He walks to a tree and hits it with his hand. Hit, hit, hit. It's very slow. After a long minute, a block of wood falls on the sand. One block isn't much, so Phuc hits the tree again and again.

Now he has got eight blocks of wood. He makes a table, but he doesn't know the shapes for tools. He puts the wood on the table in a lot of different shapes. A line. A square. A big T. Only the T works. It's a wooden pickaxe!

Phuc is hungry. There are some chickens near the trees, but they run fast and Phuc runs slowly. Under a tree, he finds two red apples. He eats them quickly. Two apples aren't much, but they're food.

Then he looks at the sky. The sun is going down! The sky is orange, then red.

"No, no, no!" Phuc says. He hasn't got a house.

He runs to a small hill and digs a hole in it with his pickaxe. He goes in and closes the hole with dirt. Now he is in a very small room. It's very dark. He can't see his hands.

Then he hears sounds outside.

*Grrr...* That's a zombie.

*Clack, clack...* That's a skeleton.

*Sss...* Phuc doesn't know that sound.

*BOOM!*

There is a big hole in his wall. A zombie is standing in the hole. Its face is green and its eyes are empty.

Phuc hasn't got a sword. He hits the zombie with his pickaxe. Hit, hit, hit. The zombie hits him too. Now Phuc has got only one heart.

Hit! The zombie falls. Phuc quickly puts dirt in the hole. He is shaking, and his heart is beating fast.

"I'm alive," he says quietly. "I'm alive."

He sits in the dark. He doesn't sleep, because he hasn't got a bed. He is cold, hungry and alone.

Then he digs a little in the wall, and he sees black spots in the stone. Coal!

Phuc has got coal, and he has got a stick. He puts the coal on the stick. Now he has got a torch! He puts it on the wall, and the small room is yellow and warm. It isn't a house, but it's his.

In the light, he can see the walls. There is some coal, and there is a little iron too. Phuc is smiling.

In the morning, the zombies are on fire in the sun. Phuc opens his door carefully. The world is quiet and green again.

He has got one torch, one pickaxe, some coal and a little iron. He hasn't got a house or a bed, and he is very hungry. But now he knows the T shape. He knows the zombie's sound. And he knows the creeper's sound too.

The world is very big, and Phuc is very small. But he isn't afraid now. Well, he is a little afraid.

He looks at the trees and smiles.

"Okay," he says. "I need wood."
